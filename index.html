WHY FAMILIES CHOOSE AMJAD

Built on six commitments

These are the values our teachers, staff, and families return to every single day.

✦

Faith-Centered Learning

Daily Qur'an, Islamic studies, and prayer are woven into the rhythm of every school day, not treated as an add-on.

✦

Academic Rigor

A provincially aligned curriculum taught by certified teachers, with small class sizes and individual attention.

✦

Community & Character

Students graduate with strong character, service habits, and a lasting sense of belonging to their community.

◆

Bilingual Foundation

Arabic language study begins in Pre-K, building fluency and confidence alongside a strong English program.

◆

Modern Facilities

Renovated science labs, a dedicated STEM & robotics room, and a full-size gymnasium support hands-on learning.

◆

Family Partnership

Regular parent–teacher touchpoints, a family handbook, and an active Parent Association keep families close to school life.
