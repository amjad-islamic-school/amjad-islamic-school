What does this page do?

This shows the class you selected when you registered as a teacher, along with an "Open Classroom" button that takes you straight to your class. If the button isn't working yet, it means the administration hasn't added the classroom link for your class.

Important Notice

Your account and classroom link are confidential and exclusive to you. Sharing your login details or the classroom link with anyone outside the school is not permitted. Safeguarding this information protects the rights of all registered students.
