Amjad Institute of Islamic Studies for Women

Advanced Islamic education for women after high school — a comprehensive four-year program combining Qur'an, Hadith, Fiqh, Arabic Language, and Da'wah, preparing every student academically, spiritually, and as a future leader.

Four-Year Program 8 Semesters Monday to Friday Women-Only Program

The Institute offers a complete four-year academic journey — beginning with the Islamic foundations, deepening into Qur'an, Hadith, and Arabic sciences, advancing into the higher Islamic sciences, and concluding with leadership, da'wah, and contemporary Islamic thought.
